about me

cyberspezie

Creative Technologist & Multidisciplinary Artist

I am (aka cyberspezie), born in 2004. Growing up with a programmer as a father, I developed a deep love for the machine and industrial aesthetics, a fascination that today defines my dark and minimal visual identity.

With a computer science diploma at I.T.I. G. Marconi (Dalmine) and on going studies in New Technologies of Art at Brera Academy of Fine Arts, my work merges a scientific approach with a humanistic one, creating a continuous dialogue between rigor and instinct. Driven by the curiosity to understand systems from the inside out, I combine my IT background with artistic research to autonomously manage the entire lifecycle of digital products, installations, and phygital experiences.

I take care of every aspect: from design and art direction (UI/UX, layout, visual conception), to the software core (coding, API integration), down to the hardware skeleton (physical computing, electronics, circuit soldering). By making the most suitable tools communicate with each other, I build hybrid architectures with a single underlying desire: to create ecosystems where the organic and the artificial can harmoniously coexist.
